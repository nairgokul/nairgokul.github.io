      Paid AI tools and pricing

      The two most common AI subscriptions are OpenAI's ChatGPT and Anthropic's Claude. Tiers 3 and 4 have 10x and 20x the quotas of Tier 2, respectively.

      VendorProductFree$8/month$20/month$100/month$200/month

      Other options for faculty

      These programs provide a PI with access to premium AI tools that can be shared with students and postdocs in their group.

      Purchasing AI using grant funds

      All AI purchases must be made through the university, not with personal funds, and coordinated with LSA Technology Services.

      1. 1

        Contact LSA Technology Services

        Reach out to Technology Services or use the Software Purchase Form.

      2. 2

        Provide purchase information

        List the software and licenses to purchase and the funding shortcode.

      3. 3

        Assess accessibility and security

        Include an accessibility risk assessment and explain what data will be shared with the software. Review examples of sensitive data by classification level and the Sensitive Data Guide to IT Services.

      4. 4

        Complete the purchase with LSA

        LSA Technology Services may contact you to discuss security in more detail. They will then arrange a meeting with you to purchase the subscription.

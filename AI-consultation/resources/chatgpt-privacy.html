Privacy settings on ChatGPT

Thanks to Kartik Prasanna for pointing this out on the discord: ChatGPT's privacy policy allows them to train on your data even if you have turned off "Improve the model for everyone" in settings. If you want OpenAI to not use your chats for training their models, you shoud follow these steps:

  • Go to the link OpenAI Privacy Portal.
  • Click on "Do not train on my content".
  • Click the checkbox, choose your region and press "submit request."
  • You will receive an email from OpenAI saying that they received your request and a few minutes later another email saying they have successfully processed your request.

Note that this only prevents OpenAI from training on your *new* chats. The request does not apply to older chats.
